Clothing Diary

A clean and intuitive outfit tracking app for iPhone and iPad.

Calendar View

Your home screen. Track outfits at a glance, see daily markers, and jump to weekly summaries or editing tools.

Add Clothes

Register clothes with a photo, category, purchase date, and a quick note.

Edit Clothes

Update item details anytime. Keep your wardrobe clean and organized.

Select Outfit

Choose clothes for the selected date from your wardrobe.

Weekly View

Review outfits for the week in a simple list format.

Features

Calendar integration (EventKit)
Camera capture & quick photo edit
Custom categories
Notes & purchase date tracking

How to Use

  1. Add your clothes with photos and categories.
  2. Select a date on the calendar.
  3. Pick clothes for that day.
  4. Open Weekly View to review your outfits.
